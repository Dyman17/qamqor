## What follows from this

1. **Immersive 3D/WebGL is the strongest signal**: all six studio sites were liked. A full-screen 3D scene (collar, terrain, particles) with scroll-driven direction is wanted more than a hero picture with cards.
2. **Cinematic product, not a spec sheet**: cars (Rivian, Lucid, Polestar), Leica and B&O were liked. Pages packed with specs and comparisons (MacBook Pro, Dyson) were disliked. Large photography and video, few words, restraint.
3. **Dark, precise UI**: Linear, Raycast and Framer were liked; Stripe, Vercel and Supabase, with gradients and a «dev» feel, were not.
4. **Agro sites should be human and outdoors**: Halter (photos of the field and people) was liked; corporate Vence was disliked.
5. **Character and playfulness are welcome in small doses**: Playdate, TE EP-133, Nothing.

## Direction for landing v4 (proposal)

- **Hero**: a full-screen WebGL scene. The collar on the low-poly steppe terrain of Mangystau at dusk; the camera moves on scroll (fly-in, orbit, close-up of the solar panel and GPS). The virtual fence is a glowing line on the terrain, with herd points around it.
- **Scroll storytelling**: 4–5 scene «chapters» (problem → how it works → zones and signals → app → install) instead of a ribbon of card sections.
- **Typography**: large confident sans-serif with tight tracking. Few words, one accent colour against dark green and graphite.
- **Photography**: full-bleed, cinematic, in the dark green tone. No collages.
- **Fewer cards and tables**: the comparison and specs are moved into a collapsible block.
- **Icons**: Phosphor only.
